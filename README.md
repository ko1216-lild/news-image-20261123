A program that writes one image summing up the world news of Monday 23 November 2026: a front-page poster led by Bahrain's demand for UN action after an Iranian attack on a civilian facility, with panels on the Indonesia factory fire that killed three and left 36 missing, South Korea's education chief resigning over AI cheating, Kim Jong Un's remark that North Korea seeks security not supremacy, Myanmar's pledge to eliminate graft in military recruitment, China's condemnation of the US ban on its telecom companies, and protests in Turkey over the closure of pro-opposition media outlets. The poster is composed by compose.py (Pillow) into entry.png; main.py writes it to the output directory, byte for byte.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
